BlueAsh 2.24.1 online update deployment (HTTPS).
Stable feed: https://modepulse-relay.modepulse.workers.dev/update/manifest.json
Beta feed: https://modepulse-relay.modepulse.workers.dev/update/beta.json
Build, then run source/release.ps1 to publish beta first; verify installation on the developer PC before -Promote.
The feed contains version, notes, size, SHA-256, URL and 8 MiB part paths.
The online archive keeps ModePulse/ModePulse.exe for old updaters; BlueAsh installs it as BlueAsh.exe.
The app validates archive paths, executable version and SHA-256 before requesting graceful shutdown.
Replacement/startup failures attempt rollback to the preserved previous executable.
The public server supplies the ZIP and manifest; their hash is an integrity check, not a digital signature.
